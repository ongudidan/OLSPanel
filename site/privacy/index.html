Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 12, 2026

1. Overview

OLSPanel is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we may collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your data.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

OLSPanel does not collect or store any personal data from users by default. The software runs entirely on your server, and no data is transmitted to our servers.

3. Optional Data Collection

In the future, if optional features are added (such as update checks, analytics, or community plugins), you will be clearly notified, and opt-in will be required. We will never collect data without your consent.

4. Server and System Logs

OLSPanel itself does not log personal information. However, underlying software such as your web server or operating system may generate logs. These logs are managed entirely by you and are not accessible by us.

5. Use of Cookies

OLSPanel does not use cookies. If you're visiting our website (e.g., olspanel.com), basic analytics tools may use cookies to help us improve the site, but no personally identifiable information is collected.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to ensure that any optional data shared with us (such as through contact forms or email support) is handled securely and not disclosed to unauthorized parties.

7. Third-Party Services

OLSPanel integrates with third-party tools like OpenLiteSpeed, PHP, MariaDB, etc. These tools are governed by their own privacy policies and do not report any data to OLSPanel.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@olspanel.com.
