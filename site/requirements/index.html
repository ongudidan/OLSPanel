System Requirements

Ensure your server is ready for OLSPanel with these requirements.

Minimum Requirements

Before installing OLSPanel, ensure your server meets the following minimum requirements to guarantee optimal performance and compatibility.

Operating System

  • Ubuntu 20 or 22 or 24
  • Debian 11 or 12
  • Almalinux 8 or 9
  • CentOS Stream 8 or 9
  • Rocky Linux 8 or 9

Note: OLSPanel does not support Windows servers. For Windows-based hosting, consider using a virtual machine with a supported Linux distribution.

Hardware

  • CPU: 1 GHz or higher (multi-core recommended for better performance)
  • RAM: Minimum 1 GB (2 GB or more recommended for production environments)
  • Disk Space: At least 10 GB of free disk space (SSD recommended for faster performance)

Network

  • Stable internet connection for installation and updates
  • Public IP address or domain name for accessing the panel
  • Ports 80 and 443 must be open for HTTP, HTTPS, and OLSPanel access

Additional Recommendations

For the best experience with OLSPanel, consider the following recommendations:

  • Use a clean server installation to avoid conflicts with existing software.
  • Enable automatic updates for your operating system to ensure security patches are applied.
  • Configure a firewall (e.g., UFW) to secure your server, allowing only necessary ports.
  • Regularly back up your server to prevent data loss.
